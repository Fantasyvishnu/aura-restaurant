The Journey & Vision

Our Heritage & Philosophy

A love for Tamil Nadu's kitchens, from temple towns to coastal villages, and for the tradition of treating every guest like family.

Tamil Heritage, Seven-Star Service

Where Ancient Recipes Meet
Royal Hospitality

AURA means "palace of beauty": a place built around the joy of a Tamil feast.

Our kitchen brings together five regional traditions of Tamil Nadu: the fiery, aromatic Chettinad cuisine, the coconut-and-millet kitchens of the Kongu region, Madurai's famous late-night favourites, the temple sweets of Thanjavur and the seafood of the Coromandel coast.

Masalas are ground on stone, curries are simmered slowly, and every meal is served the traditional way on a fresh banana leaf, in a dining hall inspired by the carved pillars and courtyards of old Tamil palaces.

"Virunthombal, the care we show to a guest, is the oldest recipe we know. Everything else is just cooking."

— Head Chef, AURA
