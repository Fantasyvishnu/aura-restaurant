Booking, Fine & Privacy Policy

Simple rules so every guest gets a table and a great evening.

Booking & Payment No-show Fine Cancellation Bar Policy Privacy

Booking & Payment

  • Booking a table on this website is free.
  • There is no online payment and no deposit. We never ask for card or bank details on this website.
  • Any amount (your bill, or a fine described below) is collected in person at the restaurant, in hand.
  • Your booking is a request until our team confirms it. We confirm by e-mail, and by phone or WhatsApp when possible.
  • Tables can be booked up to 30 days ahead, for up to 20 guests online. For larger groups please call {{ restaurant.events_phone }}.
  • We serve every day from 12:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Last seating is at 10:30 PM.

No-show Fine

  • We hold your table for {{ restaurant.grace_minutes }} minutes after your booked time.
  • If you do not arrive within that time, and you have not cancelled, the booking is marked No-show and a fine of ₹{{ restaurant.no_show_fine }} per booking applies.
  • The fine is collected at the restaurant, in hand, on your next visit. It is never charged online.
  • We send you a message (e-mail, and WhatsApp when possible) when a no-show fine is recorded.
  • If something genuinely went wrong (an emergency, for example), please call us. The manager may waive the fine.

If you are late but on your way, call {{ restaurant.phone }} and we will do our best to keep your table.

Cancellation

  • Cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your booking, from your guest dashboard.
  • Within 24 hours, please call {{ restaurant.phone }}. Our team can cancel it for you.
  • Guests who booked without an account can call us to cancel.

Bar Policy

  • Alcohol is served only to guests aged {{ restaurant.bar_min_age }} and above. Please carry a photo ID, as we may ask for it.
  • We may refuse service to any guest, and we ask guests not to drive after drinking.
  • Mocktails, juices, tea and filter coffee are available for everyone.

Privacy

  • When you book we store your name, e-mail, phone number and booking details. When you create a guest account we also store your username and an encrypted password.
  • We use this only to manage your reservation, contact you about it (e-mail, phone or WhatsApp) and answer your messages.
  • We do not sell your details. They are visible only to our staff.
  • This website does not take card or bank details.
  • To see, correct or delete your details, e-mail {{ restaurant.email_bookings }}.
